A Little Love for the Buttermilk Sourdough

“Soft inside with a golden crust and that little sourdough tang we love!”

Most artisan sourdough loaves have a thick, crunchy crust that young children find tough to chew for everyday sandwiches. We solved that by folding cultured whole buttermilk directly into our wild-fermented dough. The lactic acids yield a cloud-soft, tender crumb that squishes just right for peanut butter and honey, yet toasts to golden perfection for Sunday morning grilled cheese.

A Note from Nicole

Why We Slow Down for 36 Patient Hours

In the 1960s, commercial breadmaking made a drastic turn toward speed. Fast-rising chemical yeast replaced patient fermentation, allowing industrial factories to turn wheat flour into bagged loaves in 90 minutes flat. But speed came at a steep cost: our digestive systems never had time to handle the unfermented proteins and anti-nutrients.

When flour and water mingle with wild lactobacillus bacteria over 24 to 36 hours of cold fermentation, something miraculous happens: the bacteria pre-digest complex gluten peptide bonds, break down phytic acid (which normally blocks mineral absorption), and lower the glycemic index of the grain.

Gentle on the Belly

Many of our neighbors with non-celiac gluten sensitivity tell us they can eat our sourdough every single day without the bloating or heaviness of store bread.

No Seed Oils or Chemical Relaxers

We use exclusively grass-fed European cultured butter, extra virgin olive oil, and organic flours. You will never find soybean oil, high-fructose corn syrup, or artificial food dyes in our kitchen.

It takes us three days to make each batch of bread and toaster pastries. It is more work, but when you share a slice with your family on a Sunday morning, you can taste the patience and honest love in every bite.

The Baker’s Story

“Made by a mama, for your family.”

Hi, I’m Nicole! Merrick Mama Bakes grew out of my desire to feed my own kids real, nourishing food. Standing in grocery store aisles reading long ingredient lists full of unpronounceable stabilizers and synthetic dough conditioners made me realize how far modern bread had drifted from genuine food.

I missed the nostalgic comfort treats of growing up — warm cinnamon rolls, golden sandwich bread, and toaster pastries — but I wanted them made with ingredients I could be proud of. Four years ago, my sourdough starter “Mabel” was born. What started as weekend baking for my family quickly turned into neighbors knocking on our door asking for loaves.

When you pick up a warm paper bag from our porch in Merrick, you are getting food made with the exact same care and uncompromised ingredients I feed my own children. Thank you for welcoming our bakes to your family table.
