Signature Cottage Loaf

The Buttermilk Sourdough

"Soft inside with a golden crust and that little sourdough tang we love."

Traditional sourdough boules often feature thick, sharp crusts that children find tough for lunchtime sandwiches. By folding cultured whole buttermilk into our wild levain, we achieve a cloud-like, pillowy crumb that squishes just right for peanut butter or grilled cheese, while retaining the digestive benefits of slow fermentation.

A Note on Digestion

Why We Slow Down for 36 Patient Hours

Modern industrial breadmaking relies on rapid chemical leaveners to push loaves from flour to bag in under ninety minutes. That speed came at the expense of our health, leaving complex grain proteins unfermented and heavy on human digestion.

When unbleached stoneground flour mingles with wild lactobacilli cultures over twenty-four to thirty-six hours of cold refrigeration, the bacteria pre-digest gluten peptide chains and break down phytic acid. The result is nutrient-rich, bioavailable bread that many folks with gluten sensitivities enjoy without discomfort.

It takes us three days to handcraft each batch of boules, morning buns, and toaster pastries. It is slower work, but you can taste the patience and care in every slice.

Nicole Crites
Mama & Baker • Merrick, NY
